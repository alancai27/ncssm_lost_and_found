Privacy

NCSSM Lost & Found is a student-run CS TA project. It is not affiliated with NCSSM, and the school does not operate or administer it.

What this site stores

When you sign in, Google tells this site your name and your @{{ auth_domain }} email address. Nothing else: not your password, not your contacts, not anything else in your Google account. When you post a found item, it stores the photo you upload along with whatever you type: where and when you found it, any note, and an optional contact method. If you mark an item as returned, it records that your account did so.

Who can see it

Item photos, descriptions, and the finder's contact details are visible to anyone who can reach this site, so that someone searching for a lost item can get in touch. Do not post a photo of anything you would not want shown publicly: an ID card, a screen with private information on it, or a document. If you would rather not publish your email, put a location such as "front desk" in the contact field instead; it is used in place of your address.

Photos are read by an AI service

To make items searchable by description, each uploaded photo is sent to Google's Gemini API, which writes out what the object is. Your search text is sent the same way when you look something up. Those requests go to Google and are handled under Google's terms, not this site's. No name or email address is ever sent with them.

How long it is kept, and removal

Items stay listed until someone marks them returned. Returned items remain visible under "see returned" so people can tell an item was claimed. To have a post or a photo deleted for any reason, contact the maintainer below and it will be removed.
